# InfluenceFlow AI – Sample Email Templates

## 1. Initial Collaboration Email

**Subject:** Collaboration Opportunity with [Brand Name]

Hi Aisha,

I hope you're doing well!

We are reaching out from [Brand Name] to explore a potential collaboration with you.

Your profile in the fashion niche appears relevant to the type of creators we are looking to connect with.

We would love to discuss a possible collaboration and explore how we could work together.

Would you be interested in discussing this opportunity?

Looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,  
[Your Name]  
[Brand Name]

If you are not interested, please let us know and we will not contact you again.

---

## 2. Follow-up Email

Best regards,
